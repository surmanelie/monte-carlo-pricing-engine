## Validation table

Every Monte Carlo estimator is compared with an independent reference. *Error (SE)* is
$(\hat V - V_{\mathrm{ref}})/\mathrm{SE}$. *In 99 % CI* checks
$|\hat V - V_{\mathrm{ref}}| \le 2.576\,\mathrm{SE}$. *Pass* uses the project-wide
tolerance $|\hat V - V_{\mathrm{ref}}| < 4\,\mathrm{SE}$. Seeds are derived from the case
identifiers once and never re-drawn, so with many rows about 1 % of references are
expected to fall outside the 99 % interval even for unbiased estimators.

--8<-- "results/validation.md"

## Are the standard errors honest? Coverage study

Each estimator is re-run with independent seeds. If the estimator is unbiased and its
standard error correct, the errors in SE units are approximately standard normal:
coverage close to 95 % and 99 %, mean close to 0, standard deviation close to 1.
